The two basic signals

. Dot

A short signal. One time unit.

− Dash

A long signal. Three time units.

Timing matters

  • Dot: 1 unit.
  • Dash: 3 units.
  • Between parts of one letter: 1 unit.
  • Between letters: 3 units.
  • Between words: 7 units.

How to tap Morse code

  1. Choose a letter.
  2. Read its dot and dash pattern.
  3. Tap short for every dot.
  4. Hold three times as long for every dash.
  5. Pause briefly between signals in the same letter.
  6. Pause longer before starting the next letter.

Learning advice

  • Learn the sound, not just the visual pattern.
  • Practice listening as well as sending.
  • Start slowly and gradually increase speed.
  • Practice regularly instead of memorizing everything at once.
